IMU Vibration Analysis

📡 Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU)

The IMU sensors measure acceleration and rotation rates. Excessive vibrations can degrade flight stability and increase servo wear. Optimal vibration levels are below ±5m/s² on all axes.
